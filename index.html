Course Curriculum

Every module is designed to give you practical, job-ready outputs.

Module 1: Foundations & Professional Mindset ▼

Understanding the VA landscape, identifying your service offerings, setting up your home workspace, and adopting a professional client-service mindset.

Module 2: Inbox, Calendar & Travel Management ▼

Mastering advanced email filtering, Google Calendar scheduling, travel itineraries planning, and efficient data entry protocols taught live by Coach Gerr.

Module 3: Project Management & Communication Tools ▼

Hands-on practice with Asana, Trello, Notion, Slack, Zoom, and Loom to handle team coordination and async task updates effectively.

Module 4: Resume, Portfolio & Client Outreach Strategy ▼

Building a client-attracting resume, designing your digital portfolio utilizing portal resources, creating Upwork/OLJ profiles, and writing personalized application proposals.

Meet Coach Gerr

Empowering aspiring Virtual Assistants with practical training, real support, and real opportunities.

Virtual Assistant Mentor & Founder

Almost 7 years ago, Coach Gerr made a life-changing decision to leave her stable career as a government worker. Her main dream was simple: to gain schedule flexibility and spend more quality time with her family and her beloved dog. Taking that leap into the remote work space opened doors she never imagined, and she hasn't looked back ever since.

Today, a vast majority of her students go on to find incredible success after going through her live academy training. However, Coach Gerr believes in keeping it real: while she is fully committed to holding your hand through your crucial first steps as a newbie Virtual Assistant, your long-term success is ultimately driven by your own grit, accountability, and hustle.

Ready to start your VA journey? Learn with Coach Gerr and take your first step toward becoming a confident, work-ready Virtual Assistant.

The GVA Mission
