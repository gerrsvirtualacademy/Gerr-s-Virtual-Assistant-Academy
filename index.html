Kierza Garcia’s VA Journey

10 Years Travel & BPO Industry Experience

Hello VA Friends, I want to share my VA Journey

My VA Journey

My Virtual Assistant journey started with something I didn’t expect. One day, I was contacted on Upwork by a CEO who owns a travel and tours company based in Mexico. Their tours are mostly for travelers coming from the United States and Canada. Even though my Upwork account was still new, and I hadn’t bought any connects to apply for jobs, they found me.

"That moment was proof that having the right skills, clear communication, and the willingness to help can open doors."

I believe what caught their attention was my 10 years of experience in the travel and tourism industry. Over the years, I have worked as a travel agent, sales agent, and travel advisor, helping clients with tour offers, hotel reservations, car rentals, and other travel arrangements. I’ve also worked in the BPO industry, which trained me to handle customers with care, manage tasks efficiently, and work well under pressure. When the CEO reached out, I was confident in explaining how I could support their business and help them grow.

Now, I am working with their company as a Service and Business Assistant – Tours and Transportation (Assistant Manager). This is more than just a VA role — it’s about being part of their operations, supporting their tours, and making sure everything runs smoothly for their customers. My job involves combining my travel expertise with the tools and systems they use, and while I’m still learning about social media marketing (SMM) and other new tools, I have guidance from Coach Gerry and their team.

This opportunity has done more than just give me a client. It has also inspired me to plan for my own small business in the future — selling digital travel itineraries using Canva, focusing on destinations within the Philippines. I want to use my industry knowledge to create products that travelers will find helpful, while also growing my skills in design, marketing, and online sales.

I’m also excited for the upcoming funnel design course, which will help me create better marketing strategies for my client’s business. I believe that learning never stops, and each new skill I gain will not only benefit my client but also prepare me for future opportunities.

For me, this journey is not just about getting paid for my work. It’s about building trust, delivering results, and making my clients happy and satisfied. The real reward is knowing that they rely on me and value my contribution. I want to make sure they feel the same level of support from me as I feel from them.

This is just the beginning. My journey as a VA will continue to grow, fueled by passion, hard work, and the goal of becoming better every day. If you’re starting your own VA career, my advice is to make your profile stand out on your chosen platform. Show clients not only your skills but also your personality, communication style, and genuine willingness to help. You never know — the right client might just find you, like mine they found me.

Need to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before joining GVA.

Do I need previous VA experience to join?

No. GVA's training is designed for aspiring virtual assistants, including beginners who are starting from zero.

Do I need a laptop to join?

No. A laptop or computer is not required to join the training, but it is highly preferred for completing activities and hands-on exercises.

How long is the training?

GVA Virtual Assistant Training is a 3-day live training. The SMM Bootcamp is a 2-day live training.

How long are the training sessions each day?

Training is usually 3 hours per day, typically from 6:00 PM to 9:00 PM. The schedule is subject to change based on the coach's schedule.

When does GVA Virtual Assistant Training usually happen?

GVA Virtual Assistant Training usually happens near the end of the month. Exact dates and times depend on the scheduled batch and the coach's availability.

When does the SMM Bootcamp usually happen?

The SMM Bootcamp usually happens around the middle of the month. Exact dates and times depend on the scheduled batch and the coach's availability.

How will I receive the Google Meet link?

A Google Meet invitation/link will be sent on the day of the training.

What device do I need?

There are no specific phone requirements. Your device simply needs to be new enough and capable of handling Google Meet. A laptop or computer is preferred for activities, but it is not required to join.

How do I get my batch access code?

After you complete your payment, your batch access code will be sent to the email address you provided during registration. Confirmation emails are generally sent within the day. Because batch access codes are manually sent, there may occasionally be a delay.

What happens after I make my payment?

Complete your registration and payment, check your confirmation email, use the batch access information provided, join the official GVA Discord server, and follow the instructions there. Your Google Meet invitation/link will be sent on the day of training.

Do I need to join the official GVA Discord server?

Yes. All enrolled students must join the official GVA Discord server to receive important training and batch updates. The private Discord server link will be included in your confirmation email.

How do I access the GVA Discord server?

Create or log in to your Discord account, then check the email address associated with your Discord account. Open the verification email sent by Discord and click “Verify.” Once your account is verified, use the private GVA Discord invitation from your confirmation email to join the server.

What should I do after joining the GVA Discord server?

Go to the #lobby channel and state your batch and full name. An administrator will then add you to your appropriate batch channel, where you will receive your updates.

Do I receive a certificate?
